## Limits

- Negatively worded items are counted with a word heuristic. On the 10 validated reference items it matches the known keying for 10 of 10. The word lists were written with these reference items in view, so this is a consistency check, not an independent test. Edmondson items 3 and 5 are marked [Inference] as negatively worded in `reference/items.md`. It may still misread AI items; check flagged items by eye before relying on counts. Some replies marked their own reverse-scored items "(R)": the heuristic flags 19 of those 21 items. Those markers are the model's own labels, not a validated keying.
- Item text is kept as the model wrote it, including any "(R)" marker; remove markers before showing items to respondents.
- Overlap is lexical (shared words). It misses paraphrase, so a low score does not show an item is new.
- Words every item shares by design ("this", "team", "members") and common words are removed before comparing.
- Counts describe this pool only. Model behavior is not assured to repeat across runs or over time.
